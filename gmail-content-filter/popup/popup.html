Layout rules

One rule per line. Re-applied every time Gmail loads.

  • att: file.pdf hide that attachment card
  • hide: phrase remove the whole block carrying it
  • redact: phrase bar over the words, keep the layout
  • css: div.aQH hide everything matching a selector
  • from: a@b.com hide every message from that address; from: @b.com hides the whole domain

Keys, inside Gmail

  • Ctrl+Option+H pick something to hide
  • Ctrl+Option+E pick something to edit on the page
  • Ctrl+Option+V show what the rules are hiding; the same key, or a click on the red marker, hides it again
  • E while picking: switch between hiding and editing
  • ↑ ↓ while picking: widen or narrow the element
  • Enter choose it, Esc cancel

The three Control-Option combinations are also registered as extension commands, so Safari Settings › Extensions › Mail Layout › Shortcuts and chrome://extensions/shortcuts list them and let you rebind them. The keys above work whether or not you record anything there.

This only changes what Gmail shows in this browser. The words and files stay in the message, in the Gmail apps, and in Drive if the attachment was added there.
